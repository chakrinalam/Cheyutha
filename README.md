# 🌍 Cheyutha – NGO Support & Community Service Platform

Cheyutha is a social impact web application developed to support NGO activities, community engagement, and volunteer coordination through a digital platform.

The project was created with the goal of helping NGOs improve their online presence, connect with volunteers and donors, and simplify the management of social service initiatives.

Although the application is currently under development, the project represents the vision of building a centralized platform for social welfare and community support activities.

---

# 🎯 Purpose of the Project

The primary purpose of Cheyutha is to digitally empower NGOs by providing an accessible and user-friendly platform that helps organizations:

* Reach more people online
* Promote social initiatives
* Connect with volunteers
* Share updates and campaigns
* Manage donation and support activities
* Increase awareness about community welfare programs

The project aims to bridge the gap between NGOs and the public using modern web technologies.
